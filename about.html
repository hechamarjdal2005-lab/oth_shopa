O.Shop started as one small counter in Hay Anas and grew into a full streetwear destination — a place where people from every corner of Morocco come to find the next look before it hits everyone else.

Our story

Built in the streets

Safi has always had taste. O.Shop is our way of putting it on record.

It started with a question nobody in the city could answer: where do you buy streetwear here that actually looks current? So we built the place we wanted to walk into. A tight edit of graphic hoodies, heavyweight sweatpants, fresh sneakers, caps and premium jackets — nothing bloated, nothing outdated, just the pieces people are actually wearing right now.

What started as word-of-mouth quickly became a community. Instagram took us past 58K followers, then our WhatsApp turned into the real place where drops get announced, sizes get held and customers get answered in minutes. Today we ship to more than 60 cities across the Kingdom, with cash on delivery and no minimum order nonsense.

Same counter. Same standard. Just a bigger crowd.

What we stand for
